---
name: domain-brief
description: Produce a 20-minute, primary-source domain and challenge brief while product implementation begins, with explicit evidence and decision-changing unknowns.
---
Read CONTROL and the actual challenge. Spend five minutes locating the authoritative workflow, definitions, and required API docs; ten minutes checking the three assumptions most likely to invalidate the product; five minutes delivering docs/research/TASK_ID.md.

Deliver: user and costly step, current workaround, exact input/output, three representative cases, available API/data with terms and setup blockers, strongest competitor, one proposed measurable advantage. Label each claim VERIFIED with URL and source date, STRONG INFERENCE with its supporting observations, or UNKNOWN. Separate the source's claim from your own validation.

Prioritize facts that change scope or API feasibility. Do not produce a market essay, invent user interviews, or turn an organizer's advertised credit pool into an allocated balance. Stop at the deadline and hand off the unknowns.
